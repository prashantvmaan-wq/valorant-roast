PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

Welcome to VALORANT Roast. This Privacy Policy explains how our website handles information when you use our player analytics and entertainment platform.

1. Information We Collect

Our current prototype allows users to enter their Riot ID and tagline. This information is temporarily stored in browser session storage and used to demonstrate the application's functionality.

The website currently uses fictional sample statistics. It does not currently authenticate Riot accounts or retrieve actual Riot player match history.

2. How We Use Information

Information entered into the prototype is used to:

  • Display the entered player identifier.
  • Demonstrate player statistics and weapon cards.
  • Demonstrate the application's roast-generation features.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

The current prototype stores the entered Riot ID and tagline in browser session storage. The Riot ID is also sent to our backend to retrieve demonstration statistics.

We do not currently offer account registration or payment functionality. Our hosting providers may process technical information and request logs as part of operating the website.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted using Vercel and Render. These services may process technical information necessary to host and operate the application.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by our application. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your Choices

You can stop using the website at any time. Closing your browser session clears the session storage used by the current prototype.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application develops. Updated versions will be published on this page.
